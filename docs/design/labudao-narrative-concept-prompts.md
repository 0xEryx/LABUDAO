## Proof-based Roadmap

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: complete LABUDAO Roadmap page design concept for implementation

Primary request: Design a dedicated roadmap page for LABUDAO, a LABUBU-native, community-owned, non-profit-by-mission experimental DAO. The roadmap must explain how one real LABUBU community experiment becomes a fully open-source protocol for collector communities worldwide.

Audience: LABUBU collectors, art-toy communities, cultural stewards, open-source builders.

Exact code-native visible copy: navigation “Overview”, “Protocol”, “Governance”, “Redeem”, “Roadmap”; page heading “Prove it with LABUBU. Open it to everyone.”; supporting copy “We will not generalize the protocol before one community can prove the complete loop.”; milestone names “01 / Community Ground”, “02 / One Object, Full Loop”, “03 / Cultural Commons”, “04 / Open Protocol”, “05 / Collector Federation”; open-source pledge heading “Success means giving the tools away.”; collector examples “Sports cards”, “Anime figures”, “Goods & merch”, “Nendoroids”, “Art toys”, “Rare objects”.

Structure: quiet fixed header; editorial hero with large two-line headline and restrained supporting copy; a horizontal or stepped five-milestone rail with each milestone showing a concise proof/exit condition rather than dates; downstream full-width open-source pledge band with category list and a strong link back to Protocol. Vary rhythm with open bands and rails, not repeated cards.

Milestone proof ideas: Community Ground = public charter, contribution rules and accountable governance; One Object, Full Loop = one LABUBU completes passport, custody, governance and verified redemption; Cultural Commons = exhibitions, loans, stories and public-interest programs; Open Protocol = audited contracts, documentation, SDK and reference implementation released under an open-source license; Collector Federation = independent communities fork and govern their own versions.

Visual system: match existing LABUDAO near-black editorial site; ivory text; cool silver-blue accent only; Manrope-like modern grotesk display; IBM Plex Mono-like metadata; hairline borders; square restrained buttons; generous whitespace; no rounded card grid; no fluorescent green, no yellow, no gold, no colorful gradients.

Implementation constraints: all text, roadmap stages, buttons and nav remain code-native HTML/CSS; practical responsive multipage layout; timeline may horizontally scroll on mobile; no fake progress percentages or dates.

Avoid: official POP MART affiliation, promises of legal nonprofit registration, investment returns, token-price language, fake metrics, corporate ESG clichés, utopian stock photography, hero eyebrow pill, neon, glow, bokeh, generic crypto dashboard, excessive icons, unreadable tiny text, watermark.
