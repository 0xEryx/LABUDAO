Chapter 01 / The origin

LABUBU gave us
a place to begin.

LABUBU sits where character design, art toys, collector ritual and personal memory meet. It is not a placeholder for a generic “real-world asset.” It is the culture this DAO was created to serve.

LABUDAO begins with LABUBU collectors and one concrete question: can a community protect the object, preserve its story and decide together what should happen next?

We begin with one character, not because the future is small, but because proof must be real.

Chapter 02 / The commons

Not a fund.
Not a marketplace.
A commons.

LABUDAO is non-profit by mission. We are not here to extract value from fandom or promise token returns. We use transparent DAO governance to return cultural value to the community.

01

Protect the culture.

Preserve the stories, provenance and community meaning around LABUBU instead of reducing every object to a price.

02

Share access.

Use exhibitions, loans, gatherings and public-interest programs to let more people experience what a community protects.

03

Govern in public.

Make proposals, treasury rules, custody decisions and community responsibilities visible and accountable.

If LABUDAO creates value, that value should return to the community, the objects and the culture around them.

Chapter 06 / The gift

Prove it here.
Give it away.

LABUDAO belongs to LABUBU culture first. That focus gives us a real community, a real object and real responsibilities instead of an abstract promise.

If the experiment succeeds, the full protocol and technical foundation will be released as open-source public infrastructure for sports cards, anime figures, goods, Nendoroids, art toys and every collector culture that wants to build its own commons.
