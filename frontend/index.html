Chapter 01 / The belief

Collecting is
an act of memory.

An artwork is more than material. A collectible is more than a price. Every meaningful object carries authorship, scarcity, custody, affection and the decisions of everyone who has protected it.

Yet the object often outlives the systems that describe it. Certificates stay in drawers. Exhibition rights live in emails. Ownership changes, but context disappears.

The object deserves a history as durable as the object itself.

Chapter 02 / The fracture

The object moves.
The truth breaks.

Today, the physical object and its digital story travel through different worlds. Each handoff creates another place for trust to disappear.

01

Provenance fragments.

Certificates vanish, resale histories split across platforms, and the story starts again with every new owner.

02

Custody becomes a promise.

A token can move in seconds while the physical object remains unseen, unverified or held somewhere else.

03

Rights stay ambiguous.

To own, exhibit, lend, govern, transfer and redeem are different rights—usually compressed into one vague claim.

LABUDAO exists to keep the object, its rights and its history in one continuous truth.

Chapter 03 / The protocol

A living record.
Not a digital twin.

LABUDAO does not aim to wrap every object in a speculative token. Its target architecture connects identity, physical custody, programmable rights, collective decisions and redemption to the same evolving record.

Chapter 06 / The horizon

Begin with one object.
Build for everything worth remembering.

Labubu is our first public experiment: a recognizable cultural object, a live participation token and a governance contract on Ethereum.

But the destination is larger—a neutral protocol that an artist, museum, custodian, collector or community can use without asking permission and without rebuilding the same trust layer from zero.
