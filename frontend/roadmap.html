LABUDAO proof-based roadmap

01

Community
Ground

Align on purpose, publish the charter, define contribution rules and establish accountable governance.

Proof / ExitPublic charter, roles, decision process and non-profit commitment are live and transparent.
02

One Object,
Full Loop

One LABUBU completes Passport, Custody, Governance and verified Redemption end to end.

Proof / ExitThe community can independently verify the full record and physical handoff.
03

Cultural
Commons

Use the proven loop for exhibitions, loans, shared stories and public-interest cultural programs.

Proof / ExitPrograms go live, and their records become public, reusable community memory.
04

Open
Protocol

Release audited contracts, technical documentation, an SDK and a reference implementation.

Proof / ExitThe repository and license are public. Anyone can inspect, build and improve it.
05

Collector
Federation

Independent collector communities fork, adapt and govern versions that fit their own culture.

Proof / ExitMultiple communities operate independent instances and share improvements in the open.

Success means
giving the tools away.

LABUDAO is LABUBU-native, but the public good should not end with us. Once the complete loop is proven, the protocol and its technical foundation will be open for every collector community to study, fork and govern independently.
